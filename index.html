About Me

I'm a Staff Engineer and IEEE Senior Member with over 13 years of experience in embedded systems, wireless communications, and hardware acceleration. Currently at AMD, I architect and develop adaptive compute platforms that power next-generation data centers.

My journey spans across industry leaders including Qualcomm, Samsung, and SiriusXM, where I've contributed to groundbreaking technologies in 5G modems, audio systems, and satellite radio platforms.

I hold a Master's degree in Computer Science from Northeastern University and have authored 4 patents in wireless technologies and machine learning optimization, along with multiple publications in embedded systems and audio processing. My work has been recognized through IEEE Senior Membership for significant contributions to the field of wireless communications and FPGA design.
